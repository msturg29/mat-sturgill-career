$30Mportfolio owned

Building the vendor-spend function a global IT organization didn't have

At Pentair, owner of the IT indirect vendor renewal portfolio (~110 contracts, $30M annually) with $35M+ in annual spend approval authority and a seat alongside Finance in AOP development. The machinery, built in the first eighteen months:

  • Replaced a single-owner, 30-day Excel renewal cycle with a continuous, company-wide Jira Service Management queue, moving renewals from a two-to-three-week reactive window to proactive lead time. Escalation owner when vendor or payment issues threaten access.
  • Rebuilt software and vendor intake (OneTrust to JSM) with end-to-end tracking; request throughput roughly doubled as previously untracked demand surfaced.
  • Re-engineered the PO process from 15 minutes to 2 per PO, removed a redundant double-approval layer, and built the PO and renewal register the team runs on.
  • Led Microsoft Enterprise Agreement and Azure strategy: modeled the E7 conversion and recommended against it; negotiated the Azure commitment discount from 7–9% to 10–11% across three years on a $7M commitment.
  • Built the working prototype of an AI-enabled, risk-tiered intake program myself after a conventional build stalled; selected as a finalist in the company's enterprise innovation competition (AI category).
  • Direct a cross-functional group of six to seven across Sourcing, Security, ERP, and Atlassian administration, with no reporting lines.
